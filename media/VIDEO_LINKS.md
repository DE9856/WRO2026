# Video links — required by WRO §7

Each URL must show at least 30 continuous seconds of the vehicle driving
**fully autonomously** (no remote control, no team member touching it
once started — rule 11.6/11.7). Public YouTube link or "accessible by
link" is fine; fully private will not work for judges.

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Vehicle placed and started per the real §9 start procedure (switch
      on, then a single start button — no extra interactions, rule 9.10/9.11).
- [ ] Camera angle shows the whole track, not just the robot close-up —
      judges need to see it actually navigating, not just moving.
- [ ] At least 30 unbroken seconds of autonomous driving in the clip
      (rule §7 requirement — cutting the clip short doesn't satisfy it).
- [ ] Obstacle Challenge clip shows the vehicle obeying at least one
      red/green pillar correctly (rule 9.19) — a clip with no obstacles
      in it doesn't demonstrate the Obstacle Challenge specifically.
- [ ] Upload as Public or Unlisted (Unlisted = "accessible by link" per
      §7's wording), then paste the real URL into the table above.
- [ ] Once real links exist, remove the "❌ TODO" status and update the
      corresponding line in `readme.md`.
